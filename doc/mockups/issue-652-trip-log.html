5. Trip logging help

The ? icon in the app bar opens this shared help dialog. Short reminders remain beside relevant controls.

‹Trip log

About trip logging

HMB checks location when the app opens or resumes. It records trips over 500 m and does not track continuously.

Trip times are inferred from app activity. Review the details and classify each trip as Business or Personal.

Road distances need Google Routes to be configured. If a route is unavailable, retry after checking Maps setup. No straight-line distance is substituted.

Origin and trip classification are separate choices. Check local tax rules before claiming travel.

The origin defaults to the saved business address. Selecting “Enter an alternate address” reveals address fields; it does not change the trip’s Business or Personal classification. The rate label and unit follow the configured business currency and preferred distance unit (km or mi). This example shows miles.
