# Item pricing

Items belong to tasks and follow each task's effective billing type. Available
item types include Labour, Materials - buy/stock, Tools - buy/hire/own and
Consumables - buy/stock.

For fixed-price estimating, Labour items can use hours or money. Material and
tool costs, quantities and margins contribute to calculated charges. Where the
editor offers **Enter charge directly**, review the entered line charge rather
than assuming it remains calculated from cost. Do not multiply an already
calculated line total by quantity again.

Actual costs recorded for fixed-price work measure profitability. They do not
replace the agreed quoted charge. T&M labour comes from time entries; completed
eligible items use actual pricing for billing. Review quantities, returns,
non-billable items and prior billing before generating an invoice.
