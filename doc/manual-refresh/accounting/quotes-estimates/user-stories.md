# Pricing example

For an estimated material cost of 11 per unit and quantity 3, the line cost is 33.
A 15% markup gives a calculated line charge of 37.95. These are illustrative
figures, not prices or tax advice.

Review whether the item uses calculated pricing or **Enter charge directly**.
Fixed-price actual costs support profitability analysis; T&M actuals affect
eligible billing. See [Item pricing](quoting-a-job/elements-a-quote.md).
