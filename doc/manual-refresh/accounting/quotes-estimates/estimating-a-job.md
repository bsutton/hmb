# Estimating a job

Open **Accounting → Estimator** and choose the job. Build the estimate from tasks
and task items. Break complex work into quantities and activities you can estimate.

Fixed-price tasks can use Labour items estimated in hours or money, plus material,
tool and consumable items. Review the resulting charge, including any margin.
Actual costs later help measure profitability without rewriting the agreed price.

For Time and Materials tasks, record labour with time entries and review actual
material quantities and costs before invoicing. Do not create Labour task items
as a substitute for T&M time entries.

See [Item pricing](estimating-a-job/elements-of-an-estimate.md).

## Product suggestions demo

Material and other purchase item descriptions can show fictional product
suggestions. Review a product and sample store before adding its reference to
the item. Existing quantities and prices remain unchanged. Prices, stock and
aisle/bay details are fixtures, not live Bunnings data or confirmed availability.
