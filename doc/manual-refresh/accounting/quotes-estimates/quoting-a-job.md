# Quoting a Job

Create a job, add fixed-price tasks and estimate their items. You can use a job
billing default or task overrides; changing every task to Fixed Price is not
required for a mixed job.

For “Paint a room”, break fixed-price labour into preparation, coats and cleanup,
and add materials with quantities, costs and charges. Review the quote scope
before generating a PDF. Record customer approval through the quote actions
before attempting to invoice quoted work.

See [Item pricing](quoting-a-job/elements-a-quote.md) and
[Quoting](../quoting.md).
