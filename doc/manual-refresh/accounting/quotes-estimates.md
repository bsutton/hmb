# Quotes and estimates

An estimate is your calculation of the work. A quote is a fixed-price proposal
with a tracked lifecycle. Use the Estimator to build task/item costs and charges.

Tasks inherit the job billing type unless overridden. Quotes contain fixed-price
scope; T&M invoicing uses recorded time and eligible material actuals. Do not
assume all tasks in a mixed job belong to the same quote.

See [Quoting](quoting.md), [Quoting a Job](quotes-estimates/quoting-a-job.md), and
[Estimating](quotes-estimates/estimating-a-job.md).
