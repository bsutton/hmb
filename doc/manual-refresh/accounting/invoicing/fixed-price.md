# Fixed Price

Fixed-price billing follows a task's effective billing type. It can coexist with
Time and Materials tasks in the same job.

For tasks covered by an active quote, approval is required before invoicing.
If the approved quote has milestones, invoice that quoted scope through the
milestones. Without milestones, eligible quoted scope can be invoiced directly.
Fixed-price tasks outside the quote can be directly invoiceable.

Milestones can be drafted while discussing payment terms, but are not
invoiceable before quote approval. Review quote changes before relying on an
existing milestone schedule.

Record actual time and material costs for profitability; those actuals do not
simply replace the agreed fixed-price charges. Review the booking fee and quote
total to avoid charging the same fee again.

Additional work does not always require a new job. Add separate unquoted tasks
or use the quote revision/amendment workflow appropriate to the quote's state.
Do not assume generating an invoice automatically completes all work.
