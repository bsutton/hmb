# Accounting integrations

Configure integrations under **Home → Settings → Integrations**.

- **Xero**: invoice upload and payment-status synchronization.
- **QuickBooks**: QuickBooks Online invoice export, labelled **preview**.

Enable only the integration you intend to use and review its setup fields.
An enabled external-accounting workflow can require upload before invoice email
is permitted. Confirm the stored invoice billing contact and email address.

External service access requires connectivity and a suitable account. The
presence of a configuration screen does not guarantee a successful connection.
See [Xero](integration/xero.md). No live integration was exercised for this guide.
