# Suppliers

Open **Home → Suppliers** to keep supplier details available while planning and
shopping. The current editor includes a required name, service, description,
business number, BSB and account number, plus linked contacts and sites.
Enter only the details you need and save the supplier before relying on child
records in other workflows.

A contact records a person and communication details; a site records an address.
Their cards offer communication/navigation actions when the required details and
platform support are available. Recording bank details does not initiate a payment.

Select a supplier on relevant task items to help filter the [Shopping List](shopping-list.md).
Receipts can record the supplier too, supporting expense and supplier-spend review.
A supplier record is separate from a customer billing contact.
