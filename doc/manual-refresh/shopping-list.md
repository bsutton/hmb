# Shopping List

Open Shopping from the home dashboard. Items come from task items of type
**Materials - buy**, **Tools - buy**, **Tools - hire** and **Consumables - buy**.
Use the list's mode and filters to find what is still needed or review history.

When completing a purchase, review the quantity and actual cost. For eligible
T&M work, completed unbilled items can be included in an invoice. Fixed-price
actual costs support profitability without changing an agreed quote.

A shopping entry does not prove an item has been invoiced. Check the task and
invoice history or Accounting's Materials Billing report when reconciling costs.
