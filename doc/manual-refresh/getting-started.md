# Getting Started

The Setup Wizard collects your initial configuration. Reopen it from
**Home → Settings → Setup Wizard**. Individual pages under Settings let you
change Business, Contact, Billing and integration settings later.

1. Enter the business and contact details to appear on quotes and invoices.
2. Review Billing: hourly rate, booking fee, payment details and PDF appearance.
3. Create a customer with the contacts and site you need.
4. Open Jobs and add a job. Choose a billing default; review any task overrides.
5. Save the job and add tasks, materials, photos and time entries as needed.
6. Use Accounting for estimates, quotes, invoices, payments and reports.
7. Open Backup and establish database backup and photo sync.

See [Settings](system.md), [Jobs](jobs.md) and [Accounting](accounting.md).
