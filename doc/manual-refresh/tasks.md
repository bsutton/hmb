# Tasks

Save a job, then add tasks to describe the individual pieces of work. Tasks hold
instructions, items, photos, estimates and time entries. Choose a useful level
of detail, such as “Replace gate hinges” and “Paint gate”.

A task inherits its job's billing type unless you choose an override. Fixed Price,
Time and Materials and Non-billable tasks can coexist in the same job. Check this
before estimating or invoicing.

Task items describe materials, tools, consumables and fixed-price estimating
labour. Buy/hire items feed the [Shopping List](shopping-list.md); owned tools and
stock materials/consumables feed the [Packing List](packing-list.md).
Invoice eligibility also depends on billing type, completion and prior billing.

Use [Time Tracking](tasks/time-tracking.md) for actual labour and
[Photos](tasks/photos.md) for visual records. Material dimensions support length,
area and volume; select units and enter the relevant measurements. Change the
business unit system under Settings → Business.
