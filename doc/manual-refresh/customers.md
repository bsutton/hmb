# Customers

Open **Home → Customers** to create or find a customer. The editor includes name,
description, hourly rate, customer type, billing contact, contacts and sites.
A new customer's hourly rate is initially populated from the system default;
review the value before saving. The current form marks Hourly Rate as required,
so the older instruction to leave it blank is not the form's current guidance.

Use contacts for people and sites for addresses. Jobs can then identify the
person organising the work, the work site and the billing recipient. Contact and
site cards provide communication/navigation actions when the needed details are
present and the platform supports the action.

The customer Billing Contact provides a default for the job billing arrangement.
A job can have multiple contact roles, such as Primary Contact, Billing Contact,
Site Contact and Referrer. A contact's default role suggests a role for new job
assignments; it does not silently change existing assignments. Review the job's
Bill To customer and effective billing recipient before invoicing.

Invoices retain their selected billing contact/customer rather than following
later customer or job changes automatically. See [Jobs](jobs.md) and
[Invoicing](accounting/invoicing.md).

**Disbarred** marks a customer you no longer wish to work with. **Exclude from
mailings** is a separate setting: use it to omit that customer from mailing
selection. Neither option is a substitute for reviewing recipients before sending.
